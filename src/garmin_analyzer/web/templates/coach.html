Or let Claude read your figures here

The coach is Claude, an AI model of Anthropic. When you ask for a report, it reads your figures and goals and writes how you are doing and what to do in the coming week. The report is kept on this page. It needs an API key of Anthropic, of this instance or your own.

For that, this goes from this instance to Anthropic, each time you ask and never otherwise:

  • per day of the last {{ days }} days: sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, stress, body battery, training readiness, training load and steps, and their averages per week of the {{ weeks }} weeks before;
  • what Garmin says of your training status, VO2 max, predicted race times, and your age and fitness age;
  • your activities of the last {{ days }} days: when, the sport, time, distance, heart rate, climb, training effect, load and power, and your hours per sport of every week;
  • your goals, with the names and notes of your events as you wrote them.

Not sent: your name and email address, the names of activities, places and routes, and anything measured within a day or an activity.

Anthropic handles it under its terms for the API. You can turn the coach off again and remove the reports whenever you like.
